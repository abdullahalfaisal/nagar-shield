Nagar Shield — Location Sharing redesign

Review preview only. The app is unchanged. Tap the switch, the contacts and the map buttons. Use the state buttons to see every case.

What changed and why

  • Two cards, same pattern as Report Crime. “Your location” and “Send to”. Each has a title and one plain-text tag on the right. No pills, no borders on tags.
  • The map is part of the location card. It no longer sits pinned above everything. The whole page scrolls, so a small phone is never cramped. Zoom buttons are small and transparent, like every other map.
  • One row says where you are. Area, then coordinates and accuracy as plain text. Redetect is a round icon button on the right, not a full-width button.
  • “No location” is a calm state, not an error. A warm card says what happened. “Enter coordinates” and “Send without location” are one tap away. Sharing is never blocked.
  • Recipients in one card. The “All primary contacts” switch first, then the people in one list with round avatars, the primary star and a checkbox. The switch is on when every primary is picked.
  • The reason the button is off is in the card. The tag turns amber: “Pick someone”. No extra hint line under the button.
  • Two ways to send, clear order. Share by message is the main button. “Share with other apps” is a quiet text button under it. A problem after sending shows as one line above them.
  • Every state is designed. Ready, locating, no location, coordinate entry (with the outside-Bangladesh warning), sending, not sent, nobody picked, no contacts, contacts failed.
  • Same system as Home. Same tokens, 20 px gutters, 18 px cards, 14 px tiles, Fluent filled icons, light and dark. Every control is 44 to 56 px tall.
