Nagar Shield — Alert Details redesign

Review preview only. The app is unchanged. Tap the thumbnails, the map buttons and the red Flag button. Use the state buttons to see every case.

What changed and why

  • It starts with what happened. A compact incident icon in its own colour, the type as the title, then “12 minutes ago · Dhanmondi, Dhaka” on one line. The old screen made you scroll past the photo and the description to learn the type.
  • Photos are big, with thumbnails. One short preview, and a row of small thumbnails under it when there is more than one. Tap to open full screen. A video shows a play button. A report with no media shows no media block at all.
  • The description is a clear card. Larger text, easier to read. A report with no description shows no empty card.
  • The map is part of the story. A card called “Location” with a draggable map, the pin in the incident’s colour, and the area name under it. A report with no coordinates shows no map card.
  • Time in one small card. “How long ago” and “exact time” as two plain rows. Fewer boxes than before: four cards, not six.
  • Flag is clear but quiet. One red-tinted button near the bottom, with the civic note under it. The note still says this is a citizen report, not a verified incident.
  • The flag sheet is shorter. Five reasons with round radios, an optional note, then Send and Cancel side by side.
  • Every state is designed. Loading skeleton, load failed with Try again, “no longer available”, no media, no description, no map.
  • Same system as Home. Same tokens, 20 px gutters, 18 px cards, 14 px tiles, Fluent filled icons, light and dark. Every control is 48 to 56 px tall.
