Nagar Shield — Home redesign

Review preview only. The app is unchanged. Light and dark show the same layout; the phone's own setting picks the theme. Scroll inside each phone.

Incident type icons

What changed and why

  • One clear order. Who and where you are, then Emergency help, then Report, then shortcuts, then the live feed.
  • Emergency banner is the app's own. Same line siren, same flat red, size, radius and shadow as the banner in the app today. It opens the help screen and never dials by itself.
  • All four shortcuts in one row. Report, Location, History and Contacts sit together in a single card. Report has a solid violet icon, so it reads as the main action.
  • Clear names. Report Crime, Location Sharing, Crime History, Emergency Contact.
  • Plain, serious icons. Filled Fluent and Material Design icons on flat tinted tiles. Precise and neutral, not playful. No gloss and no gradients.
  • Incident icons show the event. A masked person for theft, a person kicking for assault, a warning chat for harassment, a knife for robbery, a car collision for accident, a flame for fire, a heart-pulse for medical, a person with an exclamation mark for fraud, a hammer for vandalism, an eye for suspicious. Colours are the app's own category accents.
  • Current place shown. A pill shows the detected area and GPS accuracy. This reuses the location the app already has.
  • Feed cards show the evidence. A photo or video preview sits under the description, so a card is worth opening.
  • Contrast. White text on both gradients is 4.8:1 or better. Secondary text is 6:1 or better in both themes. Control outlines use a stronger colour than today.
  • Tap targets. Every control is 44 to 48 px or taller. Chips are 44. Arrows and flags are 48.
  • Bangla. Looser line height for Noto Sans Bengali, and no letter-spaced capitals.
  • Not added. No crime statistics, charts, rankings or heatmaps, and no new tab bar. The app keeps its current navigation.
