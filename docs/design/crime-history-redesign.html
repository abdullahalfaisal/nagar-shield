Nagar Shield — Crime History redesign

Review preview only. The app is unchanged. Tap the filter card, the tabs and the rows. Use the state buttons to see every case.

What changed and why

  • One card says what you are looking at. The area, then the radius, the time window and the incident type in one quiet line. Tap it to change them. It replaces the long scope sentence at the bottom of the page.
  • Three tabs, not four. Reports, My reports, Summary. “Totals” and “Distribution” were two tabs that both showed counts; they are now one Summary tab.
  • Rows read like the Home feed. The same incident icon and colour, the type as the title, then time and area. The distance sits on the right as plain text. Your own reports say “Your report” in violet.
  • Grouped by day. Today, Yesterday, Earlier. One card per day, thin dividers between rows. Easier to scan than a stack of separate boxes.
  • The map is one tap away. A map button sits in the top bar. The full-width “Map view” link under the list is gone.
  • Summary stays counts only. Total, Yours and Nearby as three big numbers, then a count per incident type in that same fixed order. No charts, no bars, no heatmaps, as the product rule says.
  • The filter sheet is easier to use. Radius is a 3×2 grid, the time window is one row, and incident types are a tidy 3×4 grid of equal tiles, each with its own icon, in a fixed order (everyday crimes, then emergencies, then the rest) instead of a long checklist. Reset and Apply sit side by side; the separate Cancel button is gone, the scrim and Back close it.
  • Every state is designed. Loading skeleton, no results, no own reports, load failed with Try again, location unavailable (an amber card says it is showing Dhaka), and load more with “Showing 50 of 120”.
  • Same system as Home. Same tokens, 20 px gutters, 18 px cards, 14 px tiles, Fluent filled icons, light and dark. Every control is 44 to 56 px tall.
